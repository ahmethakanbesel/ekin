## Look
- Pastel backgrounds that change colour per point; rounded blob characters (circle head, rounded body) with simple dot eyes.
- Feelings shown as icons near the character: a small cloud, a heart, a spiral, a sparkle.

## Typography
- Big numbered cards ("1", "2", …) in Nunito 800 with the point's short title.

## Motion
- Bouncy but gentle: characters squash and stretch slightly, icons float up, cards slide in with `outBack`.

## Structure and pacing
1. Open with a relatable question ("Have you ever…?").
2. Three to five numbered signs or tips, each with a tiny scene.
3. A short reassuring close and a reminder that a professional can help if it persists.

## Narration voice
Kind, validating, second person; never diagnoses; avoids absolute claims.

## Captions
Middle-bottom, Nunito 800, dark plum (#355C7D) on a white rounded pill.
