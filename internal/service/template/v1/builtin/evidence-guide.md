## Structure
1. Open with the reader's situation in two short paragraphs; state what the article gives them.
2. Three to six sections with `##` headings phrased as answers, not labels.
3. Each section: the principle, the evidence (named study or guideline in prose), then what to do Monday morning — a short list or checklist.
4. A closing section with limits and when to seek supervision or refer.

## Voice
Professional, respectful of the reader's expertise, concrete; no hype, no product pitch.

## Formatting
Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences), bold only for the one key phrase per section, one table if comparing options.
