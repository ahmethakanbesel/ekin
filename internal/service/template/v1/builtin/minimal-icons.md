## Look
- Pure white background, black line icons and simple stick figures, one red accent for "the important thing".
- Diagrams: boxes and arrows, Venn circles, flowcharts, numbered lists that tick off.

## Typography
- Section numbers and titles in Oswald 600, all caps, large.

## Motion
- Fast, crisp transitions: icons slide in from the edges, lists check off with a tick, flowcharts light up step by step.
- Cut on every new sentence; nothing stays on screen longer than it is spoken about.

## Structure and pacing
1. Hook with a counter-intuitive claim.
2. "There are three things…": numbered sections, each a mini-explainer.
3. Wrap with the one rule to remember.

## Narration voice
Brisk, precise, dry humour; short declarative sentences.

## Captions
Bottom-middle, Inter 800 black on white; current word red.
