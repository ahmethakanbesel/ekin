## Look
- Flat vector shapes only: circles, rounded rectangles, simple SVG paths. No outlines, no gradients except a soft radial glow behind the focal object.
- Deep navy background (#0B1D3A → #19325C) with 2–3 saturated accents per scene. Every scene has one clear focal object, large and centred.
- Depth through layers: a far layer of tiny dots (stars, particles) drifting slowly, a mid layer, and the subject. Parallax: far layers move at 20% of the subject's speed.

## Typography
- Titles in Nunito 800, short (2–5 words), white, with a key word in an accent colour.
- Labels in Nunito 400 next to the object they name, connected by a thin line.

## Motion
- Everything eases (`outCubic` in, `inOutCubic` between states). Objects pop in with a small overshoot (`outBack`), never a hard cut.
- Camera moves are slow zooms and pans (transform on a wrapper) that travel between scales: cell → organ → body, or person → city → planet.
- Something is always gently alive: breathing scale (±2%), drifting particles, blinking stars.

## Structure and pacing
1. Hook: an intriguing question or a striking scale fact (first 2 s for shorts).
2. Zoom in or out to the scale where the mechanism lives.
3. Explain the mechanism in 2–3 visual steps, one idea per scene.
4. Zoom back out to what it means for us.
5. Close with a calm, reflective line.
Scenes last 4–7 s; long videos group them into chapters with a title card.

## Narration voice
Curious, warm, quietly amazed; plain words, short sentences, occasional "we". Never sensational.

## Captions
Bottom-middle third, Nunito 800, white with the current word in the warm accent (#F2A541).
