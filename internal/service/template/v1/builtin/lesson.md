## Look
- Soft "painted" scenes: layered flat shapes with low-contrast radial gradients for light, warm cream skies, muted earth tones.
- People are simple silhouettes or rounded figures without faces; emotion comes from posture and colour.
- Each scene is a stage: a horizon line, one or two figures, a few props.

## Typography
- Chapter and key-term cards in Lora 700 on cream (#FAF3E0); the key concept named once, clearly, in its own card.

## Motion
- Gentle slides and cross-fades between scenes; figures move along simple eased paths.
- When a concept appears, show it as an object in the scene (a scale, a doorway, a path splitting).

## Structure and pacing
1. Open on a character in a situation that poses the question.
2. Follow the character; reveal the concept through what happens.
3. Name the concept, explain why it works.
4. Return to the character with the new understanding; end on an open question.

## Narration voice
Storyteller: second person or a named character, warm, patient, no jargon without explanation.

## Captions
Middle-bottom, Nunito 800, dark text on a soft cream pill.
