## Look
- Deep navy background with a faint radial light; one warm amber accent (#E8A87C) for the key idea, sage (#7FB8A4) for the healthy alternative.
- Abstract, non-literal visuals: loops for rumination, arrows for action, two circles for relationship, a pulse line for the body. No distressing imagery.

## Typography
- Titles in Lora 700, labels in Inter 600, plenty of space around both.

## Motion
- Slow eased transitions, small gestures (a dot travelling a loop, an arrow extending); nothing flashes.

## Structure and pacing
1. A relatable moment or question.
2. Name the concept in plain words.
3. Contrast it with what helps.
4. One reflective question for the viewer; no diagnosis, no promises, no call to book.

## Narration voice
A calm clinical psychologist: precise, warm, non-judgemental.

## Articles
Evidence-based and practical for the stated audience; short sections, checklists where useful, sources named in prose.

## Captions
Middle-bottom, Inter 800; current word amber, the rest soft grey.
