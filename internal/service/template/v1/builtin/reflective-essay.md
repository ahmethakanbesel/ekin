## Look
- Warm paper background (#F4EDE1) with soft vignettes and a light grain, like an old print.
- Images (stock photos of everyday scenes, people seen from afar) in framed panels with sepia-warm treatment (CSS filter sepia(0.3) saturate(0.8)).
- Generous empty space; one thought per frame.

## Typography
- Large Lora 700 titles; quotations in Lora 400 italic, centred, with a thin rule above and below.

## Motion
- Very slow Ken Burns zooms (2–3% over a scene), long cross-fades, no fast cuts.

## Structure and pacing
1. Begin with a familiar, slightly painful moment of ordinary life.
2. Name the feeling and the mistake we tend to make about it.
3. Offer a kinder, wiser interpretation.
4. End with consolation, not instructions.

## Narration voice
Calm, literary, compassionate; uses "we"; avoids clinical labels.

## Articles
Essay form: no bullet lists, flowing paragraphs, one section break per idea, a gentle closing paragraph.

## Captions
Bottom, Lora 700, dark brown text; the current word in the amber accent.
