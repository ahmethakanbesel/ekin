## Look
- Maps drawn in SVG (simplified country/region shapes or a stylised grid), parchment background, borders in thin dark lines.
- Routes and flows as animated dashed lines with arrowheads; places marked with pins and red circles.
- Collage moments: photos and clippings pinned to a board, connected with red string.

## Typography
- Place names and titles in Oswald 600 caps; annotations handwritten in Caveat 600 with an arrow.

## Motion
- Fly the camera across the map (translate/scale a map wrapper) between locations; draw routes as the narrator traces them.

## Structure and pacing
1. Start with a specific place and a mystery.
2. Follow the trail across places, data and history.
3. Reveal the underlying cause on the map.
4. Bring it back to why it matters now.

## Narration voice
First-person investigator, energetic but grounded, cites sources.

## Captions
Bottom on a dark translucent band, Inter 800 white; current word yellow (#F6C85F).
