## Look
- Off-white paper background (#F7F3EA) with a subtle noise texture (SVG feTurbulence at low opacity).
- Key evidence appears as "documents": cards with a drop shadow holding a quote, a headline or a statistic. Important phrases get a yellow highlighter swipe (#FFE14D) that draws left to right.
- Charts are built in SVG and draw themselves (stroke-dashoffset driven by t). Bars grow, lines trace, numbers count up.
- Cut-out photos (from stock, desaturated) in rectangles with a thin black border, never full-bleed.

## Typography
- Headlines in Lora 700, black, left-aligned, like a newspaper deck. Body labels in Inter 600.
- Source lines under every figure in small Inter 400 grey: "Kaynak: ...".

## Motion
- Mostly horizontal slides and push-ins on the current document; highlighters, underlines and circles drawn by hand-like strokes.
- One idea per beat; the camera rests while the narrator makes the point.

## Structure and pacing
1. Cold open with the surprising fact or question as a highlighted headline.
2. Context: two or three pieces of evidence (documents, data).
3. The turn: why it is not what people assume.
4. What it means, ending on one sentence the viewer can repeat.

## Narration voice
Clear, confident, journalistic; attributes claims to sources; no hype.

## Captions
Lower third on a black band with white Inter 800 text; the current word turns yellow.
