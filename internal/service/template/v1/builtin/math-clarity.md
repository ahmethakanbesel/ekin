## Look
- Near-black background. Thin, precise SVG strokes (2–3 px) for axes, arrows, grids and shapes.
- Colour means something and stays consistent: each concept gets one colour (e.g. blue = what we measure, brown = what we feel), introduced the first time it appears.
- Labels and small formulas in JetBrains Mono; italic Lora for named concepts.

## Motion
- Shapes morph into each other (interpolate SVG path points or transforms) instead of cutting.
- Build diagrams incrementally: draw a stroke, then label it, then move it. Never show the finished diagram first.
- Slow, deliberate timing; hold on the key frame for a beat after each reveal.

## Structure and pacing
1. Pose a concrete puzzle.
2. Build intuition with a simple picture before any terms.
3. Generalise one step at a time, each step visibly derived from the last.
4. Restate the insight in one picture.

## Narration voice
Thoughtful, precise, inviting the viewer to think ("notice that…", "what if…").

## Captions
Bottom, small white Inter, the current word in yellow (#F4D35E); keep them clear of diagrams.
