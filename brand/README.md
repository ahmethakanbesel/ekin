# Ekin brand kit

## Name

**Ekin** (Turkish: the crop, the standing harvest of a field; also a given name).
Said *EH-kin*, two short syllables. Always written "Ekin": never EKIN, ekin or
Ekin.ai in prose. Before a vowel-sound article it takes "an": "an Ekin workspace".

The name carries the product's idea: a team plants ideas, documents and search
opportunities; Ekin grows them into videos, shorts and articles on a schedule, and
the audience is the crop that comes in season after season. Ekin is a tool for
content-led growth. It is not a video editor and is never presented as one.

## Positioning

| | |
|---|---|
| One line | Ekin turns what a team knows into a steady stream of videos and articles, and holds every piece for review before it goes out. |
| Category | Content-led growth, on autopilot with a human in the loop. |
| Not | An After Effects, Premiere or CapCut replacement; a timeline editor; an "AI video generator" toy. |
| Proof we may use | Only what the product does today (PLAN.md, PRODUCT.md). No invented customers, numbers or quotes. |

Taglines (pick one per surface, do not stack them):

- **Content that keeps growing.** (default, the landing page)
- Plant the ideas. Ekin grows the content.
- Publish steadily without living in an editor.

In the app the sidebar keeps the functional "Content pipelines".

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm (PRODUCT.md). Say what happens and what the person decides:
"Ekin drafts a 6-minute video every Tuesday and waits for your review." Avoid
hype ("10x", "viral", "revolutionary"), editing jargon (cut, reel, timeline,
studio) and AI theatre ("magic", "just press a button").

## Logo

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `mark.svg` | The sprout alone, `currentColor`, 24-unit grid with 2-unit strokes. For UI next to text, monochrome print, embossing. |
| `icon.svg` | The app icon: the sprout in Cornflower On on a Cornflower square with 8/32 corner radius. Favicon, app icon, social avatar. |

The sprout is a stem and two filled leaves, the right one larger and higher: the
next leaf is always bigger. Lockup: the icon, then "Ekin" in Geist 600 with
-0.025em tracking, cap height about half the icon, gap 0.5× the icon.

Do:

- Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the icon on every side.
- Use the mark at 16 px or larger, the icon at 16 px or larger.
- Use the mark in Ink on light grounds and Ink Dark on dark grounds when the
  square does not fit.

Don't:

- Recolour the leaves separately, add gradients, outlines, shadows or a pot.
- Rotate, stretch, or put the sprout on a film strip, play button or clapper.
- Pair it with editing imagery (timelines, scissors, reels).

Cornflower is spent sparingly: one call to action per view, the icon, links and
focus. Everything else is warm neutral, so the content (posters, video frames,
articles) carries the colour.

## Type

**Geist** (variable, 400–700) for everything; **Geist Mono** for code, commands
and timestamps. Headlines 600 with -0.025em tracking; body 400 at 1rem/1.6;
reading text (articles) 1rem/1.7. No second display face.

## Imagery

Show the work: real drafts, video posters, article sheets and the review desk,
on warm paper grounds. Growth reads through sequence and rhythm (a calendar of
published pieces, a long video fanning out into shorts), never through stock
charts going up and to the right or photos of seedlings.
